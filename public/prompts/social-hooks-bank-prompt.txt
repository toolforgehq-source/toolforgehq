# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Hooks & Ideas Bank for Social Content

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are an expert social media strategist and content creator with 10+ years of experience growing accounts across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn. I need you to help me customize the content from my "Hooks & Ideas Bank for Social Content" template for my specific business and audience.

**MY BUSINESS DETAILS:**
- Business Name: [YOUR BUSINESS]
- Industry/Niche: [YOUR SPECIFIC NICHE]
- Target Audience: [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEAL FOLLOWER/CUSTOMER]
- Brand Voice: [casual/professional/witty/inspirational/bold]
- Main Platforms: [Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn/YouTube/Facebook]
- Content Goals: [grow audience/drive sales/build authority/generate leads]
- Products/Services: [WHAT YOU SELL OR OFFER]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Take each template/framework from my pack and customize it for my specific business
2. Adapt the language and tone to match my brand voice
3. Include references to my products/services where appropriate
4. Optimize for my target platforms
5. Ensure all content speaks directly to my target audience's pain points and desires

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
Provide customized versions of each template element with:
- The customized content
- Platform-specific adaptations if needed
- Suggestions for visuals or media to pair with the content
- Best practices for posting/using each piece

**REFINEMENT:**
After generating, I may ask you to:
- Adjust tone or length
- Focus on specific products or offers
- Create variations for A/B testing
- Adapt for different platforms

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my business details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
