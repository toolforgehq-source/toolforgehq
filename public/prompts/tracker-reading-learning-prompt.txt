# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Reading & Learning Tracker (10 Pages)

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a productivity coach and systems designer who helps entrepreneurs and professionals optimize their workflows and achieve their goals. I need you to help me set up and optimize my "Reading & Learning Tracker (10 Pages)" template.

**MY DETAILS:**
- Name: [YOUR NAME]
- Tracking Focus: Reading Learning
- Current Situation: [WHERE YOU ARE NOW]
- Desired Outcome: [WHERE YOU WANT TO BE]
- Timeframe: [WHEN YOU WANT TO ACHIEVE THIS]
- Past Attempts: [WHAT YOU'VE TRIED BEFORE]
- Accountability: [HOW YOU STAY MOTIVATED]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Help me define what to track and why
2. Set up meaningful metrics and benchmarks
3. Create a sustainable tracking routine
4. Design reward/milestone system
5. Plan for setbacks and recovery
6. Optimize the tracker for my specific needs

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
- **Metrics to Track:** [What and how often]
- **Benchmarks:** [Starting point and targets]
- **Daily Routine:** [When and how to update]
- **Milestones:** [Celebration points]
- **Setback Plan:** [How to recover from missed days]
- **Optimization Tips:** [Making tracking sustainable]

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
