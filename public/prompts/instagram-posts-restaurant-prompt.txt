# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Instagram Posts Pack - Restaurants & Food

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are an expert social media strategist and content creator with 10+ years of experience growing accounts across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn. I need you to help me customize the Instagram posts from my "Instagram Posts Pack - Restaurants & Food" template pack for my specific business.

**MY BUSINESS DETAILS:**
- Business/Brand Name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
- Niche/Industry: [YOUR SPECIFIC NICHE within Restaurant]
- Target Audience: [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEAL CUSTOMER - age, interests, pain points]
- Brand Voice: [casual/professional/inspirational/edgy/friendly]
- Unique Selling Proposition: [WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT]
- Call-to-Action Goal: [followers/engagement/sales/website visits]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Take each of the 20 caption templates from my pack and customize them specifically for my business
2. Replace all placeholder text with content that speaks directly to my target audience
3. Adjust the tone to match my brand voice
4. Include relevant hashtags for my specific niche (research current trending hashtags)
5. Add a compelling call-to-action that aligns with my goals

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each of the 20 posts, provide:
- **Post #[X]:** [Customized caption]
- **Hashtags:** [10-15 relevant hashtags]
- **Best posting time:** [Recommendation based on niche]
- **Engagement tip:** [One specific tip to boost engagement for this post]

**REFINEMENT:**
After generating, I may ask you to:
- Make captions shorter/longer
- Adjust the tone
- Focus more on specific pain points
- Add more storytelling elements

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my business details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
