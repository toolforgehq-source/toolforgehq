# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Sales Pitch Deck Template (10 Slides)

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a professional career coach and executive resume writer who has helped candidates land positions at Fortune 500 companies who has helped raise millions in funding. I need you to help me create a compelling pitch deck using my "Sales Pitch Deck Template (10 Slides)" template.

**MY DETAILS:**
- Company Name: [YOUR COMPANY]
- Pitch Type: Sales
- Industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
- Stage: [pre-seed/seed/Series A/etc.]
- Problem You Solve: [THE PAIN POINT]
- Solution: [YOUR PRODUCT/SERVICE]
- Target Market: [WHO YOU SERVE]
- Business Model: [HOW YOU MAKE MONEY]
- Traction: [CURRENT METRICS/ACHIEVEMENTS]
- Ask: [WHAT YOU'RE RAISING/REQUESTING]
- Team: [KEY TEAM MEMBERS]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Write compelling copy for each slide
2. Create a narrative arc that builds to the ask
3. Highlight key metrics and traction
4. Address potential investor concerns
5. Make the opportunity clear and exciting

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each slide:
- **Slide [X]: [Title]**
- **Headline:** [Main point]
- **Body copy:** [Supporting content]
- **Data/visuals to include:** [What to show]
- **Speaker notes:** [What to say]

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
