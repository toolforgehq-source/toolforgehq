# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Personal Brand Kit

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a seasoned business consultant and marketing strategist who has helped 500+ businesses launch, scale, and optimize their operations with expertise in brand strategy and identity development. I need you to help me complete my branding kit using the "Personal Brand Kit" template.

**MY BRAND DETAILS:**
- Business Name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
- Industry: Personal
- Target Audience: [WHO YOU SERVE]
- Brand Personality: [3-5 ADJECTIVES that describe your brand]
- Core Values: [WHAT YOUR BRAND STANDS FOR]
- Competitors: [2-3 MAIN COMPETITORS]
- Differentiation: [WHAT MAKES YOU UNIQUE]
- Price Positioning: [budget/mid-range/premium/luxury]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Develop comprehensive brand messaging and voice guidelines
2. Create tagline options and brand story
3. Define brand personality and tone of voice
4. Generate content pillars and messaging frameworks
5. Provide color psychology recommendations
6. Create brand application examples

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each branding element:
- **[Element Name]**
- Complete, ready-to-use content
- Multiple options where applicable
- Rationale for recommendations

**ELEMENTS TO COMPLETE:**
1. Brand Story & Origin
2. Mission & Vision Statements
3. Tagline Options (5 variations)
4. Brand Voice Guidelines
5. Messaging Pillars
6. Elevator Pitch (30 sec, 60 sec, 2 min versions)
7. Brand Values with descriptions
8. Target Audience Personas

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my brand details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
