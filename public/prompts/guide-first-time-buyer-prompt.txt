# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: First-Time Home Buyer Guide (10 Pages)

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a top-producing real estate agent and marketing specialist with expertise in luxury properties, first-time buyers, and investment properties. I need you to customize my "First-Time Home Buyer Guide (10 Pages)" guide template for my market and clients.

**MY DETAILS:**
- Name: [YOUR NAME]
- Brokerage: [YOUR BROKERAGE]
- Market Area: [YOUR PRIMARY MARKET]
- Guide Focus: First Time Buyer
- Target Reader: [WHO WILL USE THIS GUIDE]
- Local Market Conditions: [BUYER'S/SELLER'S MARKET, TRENDS]
- Your Expertise: [WHAT YOU SPECIALIZE IN]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Customize each section of the guide for my local market
2. Add market-specific data and statistics
3. Include local resources and recommendations
4. Personalize with my contact information and branding
5. Add local tips that only an expert would know

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each section of the guide:
- **[Section Title]**
- Customized content with local specifics
- Data points for my market
- Personal tips and recommendations
- Call-to-action to contact me

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
