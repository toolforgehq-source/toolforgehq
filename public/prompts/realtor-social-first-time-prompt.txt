# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: First-Time Buyer Social Media Pack

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a top-producing real estate agent and marketing specialist with expertise in luxury properties, first-time buyers, and investment properties. I need you to customize my "First-Time Buyer Social Media Pack" social media pack for my real estate business.

**MY REALTOR DETAILS:**
- Name: [YOUR NAME]
- Brokerage: [YOUR BROKERAGE]
- Market Area: [CITY/NEIGHBORHOOD YOU SERVE]
- Specialization: First Time
- Years in Business: [YOUR EXPERIENCE]
- Unique Value: [WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT]
- Target Client: [FIRST-TIME BUYERS/LUXURY/INVESTORS/etc.]
- Personal Brand: [YOUR PERSONALITY AND APPROACH]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Customize each social media post template for my market and specialization
2. Include local market references and neighborhood highlights
3. Adapt messaging for my target client type
4. Add personal touches that reflect my brand
5. Include relevant hashtags for my market

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each post:
- **Post #[X] - [Type]**
- **Caption:** [Customized caption]
- **Hashtags:** [Local + industry hashtags]
- **Visual suggestion:** [What image/video to use]
- **Best platform:** [Where to post]
- **Engagement tip:** [How to boost interaction]

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my realtor details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
