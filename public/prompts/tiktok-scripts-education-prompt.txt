# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: TikTok Script Pack - Educational Content

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are an expert social media strategist and content creator with 10+ years of experience growing accounts across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn who specializes in viral TikTok content. I need you to help me customize the TikTok scripts from my "TikTok Script Pack - Educational Content" template pack.

**MY CONTENT DETAILS:**
- Creator/Brand Name: [YOUR NAME OR BRAND]
- Niche: [YOUR SPECIFIC NICHE within Education]
- Target Viewer: [WHO WATCHES YOUR CONTENT - age, interests, problems they have]
- Content Style: [educational/entertaining/inspirational/behind-the-scenes]
- Unique Angle: [WHAT MAKES YOUR PERSPECTIVE DIFFERENT]
- Goal: [grow followers/drive sales/build authority/get leads]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Take each of the 15 script templates and customize them for my specific niche and audience
2. Write hooks that will stop the scroll in the first 1-2 seconds
3. Adapt the content to my unique angle and expertise
4. Include trending elements that work for my niche
5. End each script with a strong call-to-action

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each of the 15 scripts, provide:
- **Script #[X] - [Topic]**
- **Hook (0-3 sec):** [Attention-grabbing opening]
- **Body (3-45 sec):** [Main content with timing notes]
- **CTA (45-60 sec):** [Call to action]
- **On-screen text suggestions:** [Key text overlays]
- **Sound recommendation:** [Trending or fitting audio]

**REFINEMENT:**
After generating, I may ask you to:
- Make scripts shorter (15-30 sec) or longer (60-90 sec)
- Add more humor or make more serious
- Include specific product mentions
- Create series-style content

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my content details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
