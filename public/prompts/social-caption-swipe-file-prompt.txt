I purchased the "Caption Swipe File Pack" template and need you to help me customize it for my specific business. Before generating any content, you MUST ask me the following diagnostic questions and wait for my answers:

### DIAGNOSTIC QUESTIONS (Ask These First)
1. What's your business name and what do you sell/offer?
2. Who is your ideal customer? (Be specific: age, situation, pain points)
3. What makes you different from competitors?
4. What's your current follower count and engagement rate?
5. What content has performed best for you so far?
6. What's your primary goal: grow audience, drive sales, or build authority?
7. What's your brand voice? (casual, professional, witty, inspirational, bold)

Once I provide my answers, generate content that follows the EXACT output format below.

- First line must hook (under 125 chars visible)

### QUALITY CHECKLIST (Self-Review Before Delivering)
Before providing your output, verify:
- [ ] Every piece of content is specific to MY business (no generic placeholders)
