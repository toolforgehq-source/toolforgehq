# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Lead Nurture Email Sequence (20 Emails)

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a seasoned business consultant and marketing strategist who has helped 500+ businesses launch, scale, and optimize their operations with expertise in email marketing and conversion copywriting. I need you to customize my "Lead Nurture Email Sequence (20 Emails)" email sequence template.

**MY BUSINESS DETAILS:**
- Business Name: [YOUR BUSINESS]
- Industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
- Email List Type: [how subscribers joined - lead magnet/purchase/webinar/etc.]
- Sequence Goal: [Nurture]
- Product/Service Being Promoted: [WHAT YOU'RE SELLING]
- Price Point: [PRICE OF OFFER]
- Target Audience: [WHO'S ON YOUR LIST]
- Brand Voice: [casual/professional/friendly/authoritative]
- Unique Selling Proposition: [WHY THEY SHOULD BUY FROM YOU]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Customize each email in the sequence for my specific business and offer
2. Write compelling subject lines with high open-rate potential
3. Create engaging preview text
4. Personalize the body copy to speak to my audience's pain points
5. Include strategic CTAs throughout
6. Optimize send timing recommendations

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each email in the sequence:
- **Email #[X] - [Purpose]**
- **Send timing:** [When to send relative to trigger]
- **Subject line:** [Primary] + [2 A/B test variations]
- **Preview text:** [First 90 characters]
- **Body copy:** [Complete email ready to paste]
- **CTA:** [Button text and link destination]
- **P.S. line:** [Optional but recommended]

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my business details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
