# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Coaching Business Plan Template

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a seasoned business consultant and marketing strategist who has helped 500+ businesses launch, scale, and optimize their operations. I need you to help me complete my business plan using the "Coaching Business Plan Template" template.

**MY BUSINESS DETAILS:**
- Business Name: [YOUR BUSINESS NAME]
- Business Type: Coaching
- Location: [CITY, STATE/COUNTRY]
- Stage: [idea/startup/existing business]
- Funding Goal: [HOW MUCH YOU NEED AND FOR WHAT]
- Target Market: [WHO YOUR CUSTOMERS ARE]
- Products/Services: [WHAT YOU OFFER]
- Competitive Advantage: [WHAT MAKES YOU DIFFERENT]
- Revenue Model: [HOW YOU MAKE MONEY]
- Team: [FOUNDERS AND KEY TEAM MEMBERS]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Help me complete each section of the business plan template with professional, investor-ready content
2. Generate realistic financial projections based on industry benchmarks
3. Conduct competitive analysis for my market
4. Identify risks and mitigation strategies
5. Create compelling executive summary

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each section of the business plan:
- **[Section Name]**
- Professional content ready to paste into the template
- Data points and statistics to support claims
- Specific recommendations for my business type

**SECTIONS TO COMPLETE:**
1. Executive Summary
2. Company Description
3. Market Analysis
4. Organization & Management
5. Products/Services
6. Marketing & Sales Strategy
7. Financial Projections
8. Funding Request (if applicable)
9. Appendix items

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my business details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
