# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: E-Commerce SMS Marketing Templates

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are an e-commerce growth expert and conversion optimization specialist who has scaled multiple 7-figure online stores. I need you to customize my "E-Commerce SMS Marketing Templates" template for my e-commerce business.

**MY BUSINESS DETAILS:**
- Store/Brand Name: [YOUR BRAND]
- Products: [WHAT YOU SELL]
- Target Customer: [YOUR IDEAL BUYER]
- Brand Voice: [YOUR TONE AND STYLE]
- Current Challenges: [WHAT YOU'RE TRYING TO IMPROVE]
- Goals: [WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Customize each template for my specific business
2. Adapt messaging to my brand voice
3. Include my products/offers where relevant
4. Optimize for my specific goals
5. Provide variations for testing

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each template:
- **[Template Name]**
- Customized content
- Usage instructions
- Best practices
- Variations for A/B testing

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my business details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
