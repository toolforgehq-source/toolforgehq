# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Creative Professional Resume Template

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are a professional career coach and executive resume writer who has helped candidates land positions at Fortune 500 companies. I need you to help me create a compelling resume using my "Creative Professional Resume Template" template.

**MY DETAILS:**
- Name: [YOUR FULL NAME]
- Target Role: [JOB TITLE YOU'RE APPLYING FOR]
- Industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
- Years of Experience: [TOTAL YEARS]
- Current/Most Recent Role: [TITLE AT COMPANY]
- Key Achievements: [3-5 MAJOR ACCOMPLISHMENTS]
- Skills: [TECHNICAL AND SOFT SKILLS]
- Education: [DEGREES AND CERTIFICATIONS]
- Career Goal: [WHAT YOU WANT NEXT]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Write a compelling professional summary
2. Transform job duties into achievement-focused bullet points
3. Quantify accomplishments with metrics
4. Optimize for ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems)
5. Tailor content for target role
6. Suggest keywords to include

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
- **Professional Summary:** [3-4 sentences]
- **Experience Section:** [For each role: achievement bullets with metrics]
- **Skills Section:** [Organized by category]
- **Keywords:** [ATS-friendly terms to include]
- **Customization Tips:** [How to tailor for specific jobs]

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
