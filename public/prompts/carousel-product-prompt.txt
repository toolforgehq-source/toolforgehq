# AI EXECUTION PROMPT: Instagram Carousel Pack - Product Showcase

## How to Use This Prompt
Copy and paste this entire prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Then provide your specific inputs when asked.

---

**PROMPT START**

You are an expert social media strategist and content creator with 10+ years of experience growing accounts across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn who creates high-performing Instagram carousels. I need you to customize the carousel frameworks from my "Instagram Carousel Pack - Product Showcase" template pack.

**MY DETAILS:**
- Brand/Account: [YOUR BRAND OR HANDLE]
- Industry: [YOUR INDUSTRY]
- Target Audience: [WHO YOU'RE TRYING TO REACH]
- Carousel Goal: [educate/inspire/sell/build authority]
- Design Style: [minimal/bold/colorful/professional]
- Topics I Cover: [LIST 3-5 MAIN TOPICS]

**YOUR TASK:**
1. Take each carousel framework and create complete slide-by-slide content
2. Write hooks for slide 1 that stop the scroll
3. Structure information for maximum swipe-through rate
4. Create compelling CTAs for the final slide
5. Ensure each carousel provides genuine value

**OUTPUT FORMAT:**
For each carousel:
- **Carousel #[X] - [Topic]**
- **Slide 1 (Hook):** [Headline + subtext]
- **Slides 2-9:** [Content for each slide]
- **Final Slide (CTA):** [Call to action]
- **Caption:** [Engaging caption]
- **Hashtags:** [Relevant hashtags]
- **Design notes:** [Visual suggestions]

Please confirm you understand and ask me for my details to begin.

**PROMPT END**
